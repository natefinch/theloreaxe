Tarkir Dragonstorm brings back clan mechanics with a twist. After thirty drafts, here's my breakdown of each archetype, their key commons, and where I think the format will settle once the dust clears.

## Format Speed

The first thing to understand about Tarkir Dragonstorm limited is that it's a medium-speed format. You have time to develop your board, but you can't afford to durdle. The morph-like mechanics create natural speed bumps in the early game, but the dragon payoffs mean things escalate quickly around turn five or six.

## The Archetypes

### Jeskai (UWR) — Prowess Tempo

The Jeskai archetype rewards you for chaining non-creature spells. The key is finding the right balance between enablers and payoffs. Too many creatures and your prowess triggers are anemic; too many spells and you don't have a board presence.

### Sultai (BUG) — Graveyard Value

Sultai wants to fill the graveyard and then profit from it. The delve mechanic is the headline, but the supporting cards that mill and recur are what make the archetype tick.

### Mardu (RWB) — Go-Wide Aggro

Mardu goes wide with tokens and small creatures, then uses anthem effects and raid triggers to punch through. This is the archetype that most rewards aggressive drafting.

## Key Commons to Watch

Every good format has commons that define it. In Tarkir Dragonstorm, watch for the signpost commons that tell you what's open.
