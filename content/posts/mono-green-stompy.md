There's a pure satisfaction in turning creatures sideways. Mono-Green Stompy might not be tier one in Modern, but it's honest, powerful, and deeply rewarding to play well.

## Why Stompy?

In a format dominated by complex combo decks and multi-color goodstuff piles, there's something refreshing about a deck whose game plan is: play big creatures, turn them sideways, win.

Stompy teaches you the fundamentals of Magic better than almost any other archetype. Every decision matters: which creature to lead with, when to use your pump spells, whether to trade or go to the face.

## The Current Build

The core of the deck hasn't changed much over the years, but the supporting cast rotates with each new set:

- **Experiment One** — Grows with every creature you play
- **Pelt Collector** — Another one-drop that scales
- **Steel Leaf Champion** — Three mana, 5/4, nearly unblockable
- **Aspect of Hydra** — The finisher that makes the deck explosive

## Sideboard Strategy

The sideboard is where Stompy gets interesting. You have access to some of Green's most powerful hate cards, and knowing when to bring them in is what separates a casual Stompy player from someone who consistently puts up results at FNM.
