MoxMox is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that makes it easy to look up card prices and track your collection while browsing Magic content online.

## Features

- **Instant price lookups** — Hover over any card name to see current market prices
- **Collection tracking** — Mark cards you own directly from any webpage
- **Price alerts** — Get notified when cards on your watchlist change price
- **Multi-source pricing** — Compare prices across major retailers

## Installation

MoxMox is available for Chrome and Firefox. Install it from your browser's extension store.
