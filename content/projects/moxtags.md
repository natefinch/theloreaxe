You know your deck needs more ramp, or another sacrifice outlet — but crafting the perfect search query is maddening. Cards that do the same thing are worded completely differently, and even a careful search will miss options you didn't think to look for.

Scryfall's Tagger system fixes this. Thousands of cards have been labeled by the community with intuitive tags like "ramp," "card-draw," "sacrifice-outlet," and "board-wipe" — labels that describe what a card *does*, not just what it *says*. The problem? Moxfield doesn't surface any of it.

MoxTags brings all of that data directly into Moxfield. No more switching between sites.

![MoxTags context menu showing art tags and card tags on a Moxfield deck](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a9f14f67-ef6a-4138-8c11-e9fd86b72ed0)

## How It Works

Right-click any card in your Moxfield deck list and you'll see two new submenus: **Art Tags** (specific to that printing's illustration) and **Card Tags** (describing the card's mechanical function across all printings).

Click any tag to instantly search your deck for other cards that share it. Check multiple tags and search for the combination — perfect for narrowing down exactly the kind of card you need.

Right-click your Commander and see it's tagged "sacrifice-outlet" and "aristocrats." Click either tag to discover which other cards in your deck share those synergies, or jump to Scryfall to search the full card database.

![Tag search results in Moxfield deck list](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d921c482-58e9-4af8-bd57-c912543548c9)

## Tag Autocomplete in Search

Start typing a tag name in any Moxfield search bar and MoxTags will suggest matching tags, so you can search by function without memorizing the exact tag names.

![Tag autocomplete in Moxfield search bar](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c048386-4015-4c1c-9389-c8e8bbcb04b6)

## Add Scryfall Tags as Moxfield Tags

You can also add Scryfall tags directly to your Moxfield cards as Moxfield tags, letting you organize your deck by function.

![Adding Scryfall tags as Moxfield tags](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fa355790-aca3-45e9-95b9-fb6eb451d246)

## Tags on Search Results Too

When searching for cards to add to your deck, MoxTags adds tag menus to both image and text search results — so you can discover synergies before you even add a card.

![Tags on text search results](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/45715033-d68d-4980-9f12-97d88e0bf501)

## Key Features

- **Art and card tags** for every card, right in the context menu
- **Multi-tag search** — check several tags and find cards matching any combination
- **Works with private decks** — reads your deck data as the page loads
- **Printing-aware** — different printings can have different art tags, and MoxTags respects which version is in your deck
- **Fast and lightweight** — data is cached locally and refreshed daily
- **No account required** — uses Scryfall's public data
