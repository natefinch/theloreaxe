TCGMizer is a browser extension that optimizes your TCGPlayer shopping cart by finding the best combination of sellers to minimize your total cost, including shipping.

## Features

- **Cart optimization** — Automatically finds the cheapest seller combination
- **Shipping calculation** — Factors in shipping costs from each seller
- **One-click optimization** — Works directly on the TCGPlayer cart page
- **Savings display** — Shows you exactly how much you're saving

## Installation

TCGMizer is available for Chrome and Firefox. Install it from your browser's extension store.
