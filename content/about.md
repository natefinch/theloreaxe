**The LoreAxe Speaks** is a blog about Magic: The Gathering strategy, deckbuilding, and the tools I build to make the game more enjoyable.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Strategy articles** — Deep dives into format-specific strategy, from Pauper to Modern to Commander
- **Deck guides** — Detailed breakdowns of decks I've built and played, with card choices explained
- **Set reviews** — Draft archetype analysis and limited format guides
- **Tool updates** — News and documentation for my browser extensions: MoxMox, MoxTags, and TCGMizer

## The Name

A lore axe cuts through the stories. This blog cuts through the noise of Magic strategy to find what actually matters at the table.
